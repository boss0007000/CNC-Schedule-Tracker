Gene Haas Advanced Manufacturing Lab

Student guide. No app or account needed: just your phone and campus email.

1Submit a project
  • Describe your part.
  • Upload a CAD file (STEP, STL or DXF).
  • Staff review it and email you an approval number.
2Reserve a machine
  • Enter your approval number.
  • Pick machine, day (up to 14 days ahead) and hour slots.
  • Cancel at least 2 hours before the start.
3Check in
  • Scan the QR code on the machine.
  • Enter the 4-digit code on the TV.
  • Wait for a supervisor to approve you.
4Check out
  • Tick the cleanup checklist.
  • Say if your tools or setup are staying in the machine.
  • Enter the TV code. Report any damage.

Be on time

Not checked in 15 minutes after your start = a strike. 3 strikes pause booking for 14 days. Strikes expire after 30 days.

Leaving something in a machine?

It shows DO NOT TOUCH for up to 1 day (tools) or 3 days (setup). Clear it out when done from My shop.

Need a slot that's taken?

Join the waiting list, or open the schedule and tap Ask for a different time.

Your account

My shop shows your approvals, reservations and strikes. Sign-in is a 6-digit code emailed to you.

Problems?

Phone dead, wrong code, or machine down: ask the supervisor on duty. In an emergency, stop all machines and follow staff instructions.

Safety first: never run a machine you aren't authorized on.
